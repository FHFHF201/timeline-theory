Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Timeline Theory Studio Uploader (the "App") handles data. The App is an internal tool operated by the owner of the Timeline Theory YouTube channel and is used only with the owner's own Google account.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the App accesses

  • youtube.upload – to upload the channel's own videos and thumbnails and set their metadata (title, description, privacy status, scheduled publish time).
  • youtube.readonly – to confirm the signed-in channel's ID and title so uploads can only go to the Timeline Theory channel.
  • yt-analytics.readonly – to read aggregate analytics for the channel's own videos (views, average view duration, average view percentage, impressions click-through rate).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the channel's own videos and to measure their performance. OAuth tokens and analytics results are stored locally on the owner's computer. The App does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect data from viewers or any other YouTube users.

Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept only as long as needed to plan upcoming videos and is refreshed at least every 30 days. The owner can delete the locally stored tokens and data at any time and can revoke the App's access at any time from Google security settings.

Cookies

The App is a desktop tool and does not use cookies. This website does not use cookies or tracking.
